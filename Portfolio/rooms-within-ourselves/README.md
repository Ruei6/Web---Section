How did the idea came to me?
I'm someone who struggles with feelings and identifying what I feel and how to deal with them so I thought why not to make a website that takes you through the emotions, introduce feelings, dealing with them and just describes everything possible. The idea of choosing a museum came to me so suddenly and it fit so well, referring to feelings as rooms in a museum that you walk in to explore it. And the most important feature that all of this is built on it is the mirror feature, you describe what you're feeling and website tells you what is it and recommends a "room" for you. That's basically all I wanted because that's my problem. Of course it's not a complicated website with tons of features and emotions it's what I'm capable of doing right now.


How I implemented the idea?
I started searching how museum websites looked like and most of them used cards and I thought that would be a great idea for my website and fits so well with the rooms. I searched through websites to know the structure and dynamics of websites generally and to make a small vision of how it should be.

The topics used.
I used HTML , CSS and JavaScript. Used visual studio code as my ide. I used these topics because to me it's the most part I was flexible at in the course and I thought I can make something with a meaning out of it.
